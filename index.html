Sketch Helper — Quick Reference

Data

Everything is stored locally on your device only. Use Export All to back up. Sketches are lost if browsing data is cleared, or when the site is closed in Incognito/Private mode (a warning shows automatically if detected).

Edit Mode

Edit Mode is where a sketch is drawn. Draw and Cut take turns holding the arrow buttons - whichever is armed decides what an arrow does, and the armed one is filled in. Tap an area to select it, or tap a corner to pick that corner and make its area the active one.

Button colours say where a button belongs: blue for Draw, orange for Cut, black for buttons that work in either. A filled button is armed, and a greyed one has nothing to do right now.

Menu

  • Tap Menu above the sketch for Quick Reference, Sketches, Settings and Report a Bug.
  • Settings holds Haptic feedback, Dark Mode, the Autolabel popup, a switch to move the arrows to the other side for each orientation, and Reset to default.
  • Report a Bug opens your mail app with what you describe, the open sketch and a picture of it attached. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Arrow buttons

  • Type a distance in the field in the middle of the pad, then tap an arrow to draw a line that way. Holding an arrow button gives other angle options in 15° increments.
  • With Cut armed, an arrow sends a cut line from the selected corner instead, and needs no distance.

Semicircle buttons

  • The semicircle button curves the last line drawn into an arc.
  • The two buttons beside it raise and lower the arc, from a shallow bow to a fuller curve.
  • Hold the semicircle button for quarter and third arcs.

Maintain arc length

With this on, curving a line keeps the line's own length, so the shape genuinely stops short of its start. With it off the line stays where it is and only the arc changes.

Action buttons

  • Draw gives the arrows to drawing. While an area is open it reads Close Area and finishes that area; while Select Multiple is armed it reads Combine and merges what is selected.
  • Add Area puts another area inside the same structure. Tap it, tap the point to start from - it turns red - then draw with the arrows or by tapping each corner.
  • Select Multiple ticks several areas at once so they can be combined or cleared together.
  • New Structure closes what is open and starts a separate structure beside it.
  • Voice Commands listens for a distance and a direction, such as "twenty right", and for "undo", "new structure", "save", "semicircle" or "circle", "semicircle up", "semicircle down", and "stop" to disarm Voice Commands.
  • Cut gives the arrows to cutting: pick a corner, tap an arrow, and the line splits the area it crosses. Two or more cut lines can be drawn at once so long as they intersect sequentially.
  • P2P Cut cuts between two points instead of along an arrow - tap the two corners, then tap Cut.
  • Add Point adds a corner part-way along a side: arm it, select a point, enter a distance and tap an arrow. It disarms once the point is placed.
  • Remove Point takes the picked corner out of a finished area. It needs no arming and greys out while no corner is picked.
  • Move Point steps the picked corner with the arrows.
  • Undo and Redo step back and forth through everything you have done to the sketch. They never touch saved sketches or their titles.

A purple outline means holding that button does something extra, and it only appears while that hold would work.

Holding a button

  • Undo - brings up Clear, which offers the selected area, this structure, or all structures.
  • Cut - punches the selected area out of the one around it, leaving a hole.
  • Add Area - duplicates the selected area.
  • Close Area - closes the shape by tracing along the areas it already touches.
  • An arrow - opens a popup with other angle options in 15° increments for that direction. The semicircle button - opens quarter and third arcs.
  • An area on the sketch - reopens it so you can carry on drawing from where it stopped.

Saving

Type a name in the title field and tap Save. Saving a sketch that came from the Sketches page asks whether to Rename the saved one or keep both with Save New Sketch.

Label Mode

The Label button beside Save swaps to Label Mode, where tapping an area selects it and the fields below name it - Order Type, Area Type, Sub-Type and an optional name. The same fields can come to you instead: turn on Autolabel popup in Settings and they open each time an area is finished. Tap Edit to return to Edit mode.

Sketches

Sketches in the Menu lists everything you have saved, with Sort By, Export All (CSV) and Delete All. Export All allows you to send a CSV file to yourself with strings for each area and their labels. This can be used in conjunction with the browser extension to automatically draw and label the sketches in MAPS if the title of a saved sketch is its policy number.
